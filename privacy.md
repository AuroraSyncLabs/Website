# AuroraSync Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 17, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the **AuroraSync** Android app ("the app"), published on Google Play by **AuroraSyncLabs** ("we", "us"). AuroraSyncLabs is an independent developer project organized on GitHub at [github.com/AuroraSyncLabs](https://github.com/AuroraSyncLabs). It is not a registered company.

AuroraSync is a remote control for a compatible ceiling light. It can also read your sleep and activity data from Health Connect, with your permission, to help you adjust your lighting and sleep routine.

## Summary

- We do not collect, receive, or store any of your data on our servers. We do not operate any servers for this app.
- The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no telemetry or crash reporting.
- Health data is read only with your permission, is processed and stored only on your phone, and is never sent to us or to anyone else.
- The only time the app contacts the internet is when you ask it to check for or download a firmware update for your light. That request goes to GitHub.

## Data the app accesses and why

| Data or permission | What the app does with it | Where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bluetooth (scan and connect) | Finds your ceiling light and sends it commands such as on/off, brightness, and settings | Between your phone and your light only |
| Internet | Checks for and downloads firmware updates for your light, only when you tap "Check for update" or "Update now" | GitHub (see "Firmware updates" below) |
| Local network access | Used only by the firmware update feature | Stays on your phone and local network |
| Health Connect (read only): steps, heart rate, sleep, active calories burned, distance, weight | Shows your data in the app and analyzes your sleep routine on your phone | Stays on your phone |

### Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth to discover and control your ceiling light. Bluetooth scanning is not used to determine your location, and the app does not request or access your device location.

### Firmware updates

When you tap "Check for update" or "Update now", the app connects to GitHub to read the latest firmware version and to download the firmware file from our public repository at [github.com/AuroraSyncLabs/FirmwareReleases](https://github.com/AuroraSyncLabs/FirmwareReleases).

As with any internet request, GitHub receives your IP address and standard technical request information when this happens. The app adds no personal data, health data, or identifiers to these requests. We do not proxy these downloads and we have no access to GitHub's server logs. GitHub's handling of this information is described in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

The app makes no other internet connections.

### Health data (Health Connect)

If you grant permission, the app reads the following data types from Health Connect: steps, heart rate, sleep, active calories burned, distance, and weight. The app only reads this data. It does not write to Health Connect.

The app uses this data to:

- show your health and sleep information inside the app, and
- review your sleep routine and suggest or apply changes to it, for example after a poor night's sleep.

This review is carried out by an AI model that runs entirely on your phone. Your health data is not sent to any cloud AI service, to us, or to any third party. Your health data itself is not sent to your ceiling light; the light only receives lighting settings.

Granting Health Connect access is optional. The remote control features work without it.

The suggestions made by the app are for general wellness purposes only. They are not medical advice, and the app is not a medical device.

### Limited use of Health Connect data

Our use of data obtained through Health Connect is limited to providing the features described above, which are visible to you in the app. Specifically:

- We do not transfer or sell Health Connect data to anyone.
- We do not use Health Connect data for advertising, marketing, or profiling.
- No person at AuroraSyncLabs can read your Health Connect data, because it never leaves your phone.
- We do not use Health Connect data for any purpose other than the features described in this policy.

## Data sharing

We do not share your data with anyone, because we do not have it.

The only third party involved in the app's operation is GitHub, which receives your IP address and standard request information when you check for or download a firmware update, as described above. No health data or personal data is included in those requests.

## Data storage and security

- The app stores its settings and any health data it keeps in the app's private storage on your phone, which is protected by Android's app sandbox.
- Firmware is downloaded from GitHub over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
- If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup. That backup is managed by your device and your backup provider (for example, your Google account), not by us.

## Data retention and deletion

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side.

Data stored by the app stays on your phone until you remove it. You can delete it at any time by:

- clearing the app's storage in Android settings (Settings > Apps > AuroraSync > Storage > Clear storage), or
- uninstalling the app.

You can withdraw the app's access to your health data at any time in Health Connect (Health Connect > App permissions > AuroraSync). After that, the app can no longer read new health data.

## Children

AuroraSync is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, we will update this page and the effective date above before or at the same time as the change is released.
